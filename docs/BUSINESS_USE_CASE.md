# Regula-AI Business Use Case (PPT single use case)

**Use Case:** An Indian MSME evaluates launching an electric kettle.

**Actor:** MSME owner / product manager.

**Goal:** Understand the questions to verify before investing in product launch and assess an indicative business scenario.

**Main flow:**
1. The user enters the product name, description, and target market.
2. Regula-AI checks whether its curated knowledge base has a relevant reference.
3. The system returns preliminary compliance guidance, evidence-status warnings, and a preparation checklist.
4. The system outlines market factors, target segments, possible channels, and growth opportunities as hypotheses to validate.
5. The user enters price, unit cost, expected sales, fixed costs, and a possible one-time compliance budget in the financial workflow.
6. Regula-AI estimates financial outcomes from those assumptions.
7. The user receives prioritized next steps: verify applicable BIS/QCO requirements from official sources, confirm testing/documentation needs, validate market demand with reliable sources, and review the financial assumptions.

**Alternative flows:** If a product reference is unavailable, the system must say so and must not invent a standard or claim certification is mandatory. If AI configuration fails, show a clear error. If assumptions are incomplete, ask the user to correct them.

**Outcome:** The business gets a structured preliminary research plan and an indicative scenario—not a certification decision or a guarantee of demand/profit.

**Important limitation:** All compliance requirements must be verified with official BIS/government sources or a qualified professional. Market and financial results are estimates.
