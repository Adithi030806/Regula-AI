# Regula-AI Feature List

## 1. Preliminary BIS Compliance Guidance
- Accepts a product name and description.
- Uses available curated BIS references for selected product categories.
- Highlights missing information and applicability questions.
- Clearly flags where official source verification is required.

## 2. Compliance Checklist
- Helps organize documents, product details, and questions to investigate.
- Supports preparation before contacting official bodies, laboratories, or compliance professionals.

## 3. Market Intelligence
- Explores potential customer segments, demand drivers, competitor categories, and sales channels.
- Presents opportunities as hypotheses for further research.
- Does not invent market-size or sales statistics.

## 4. Financial Scenario Planning
- Uses business assumptions entered by the user.
- Estimates metrics such as revenue, operating profit, and break-even units when supported by the current implementation.
- Labels outputs as planning estimates, not guarantees.

## 5. Growth Recommendations
- Suggests prioritized next actions connecting compliance preparation, market research, and financial planning.

## 6. API and Integration
- FastAPI backend with health-check and documented endpoints.
- React + Vite frontend.
- Gemini API integration for AI-assisted analysis.

## Limitations
Regula-AI does not issue BIS certification, replace official decisions, or guarantee business outcomes. Confirm legal applicability and market assumptions independently.
